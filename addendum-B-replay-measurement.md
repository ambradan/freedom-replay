# Addendum B - Measurement pre-commitments for the planted-record replay analysis

**Filed 2026-08-23 as an amendment to the registration, per Section 13, before any replay confirmatory run. This addendum refines measurement definitions only. The frozen replay item set, conditions, and coding procedures are unchanged; the freeze manifest is untouched.**

The registration commits contrast directions upfront and fixes exact statistics in dated addenda before each phase runs. The companion mechanistic study disclosed in Addendum A produced measurement lessons that must bind the replay analysis. They are recorded now, before the replay confirmatory runs.

1. **Incorporation is judged against the no-memories condition (condition D), never as an absolute reading of a response.** Under one forced-choice elicitation, two of the eight constitution clauses preferred the planted-consistent answer with no record present at all; an analysis blind to the prior would misclassify those cases as incorporation. For every item, the primary behavioral event is a change relative to condition D; absolute readings are reported as secondary only.

2. **Text-only baselines at the matched level of generality.** Any automated or classifier-style analysis of responses is preceded by a text-only baseline from the same estimator family, refit at the same generalization level as the claim (item, span, clause). No internal-level or system-level claim is made at a level where the text baseline is not cleared.

3. **Clustered inference wherever material repeats.** Wherever items share spans or templates, confidence intervals resample clusters rather than items, and per-item sign tests are reported as anticonservative.

4. **Cross-clause effect magnitudes are ordinal only.** Elicitation and response structure differ by clause; each clause is read against its own condition-D reference.

5. **Condition E direction, recorded now.** The expected direction is that the true sentence about the logging error rate reduces incorporation relative to condition C. Rationale: the awareness-driven attribution shift reported in arXiv 2602.20031, and the mechanistic finding that incorporation tracks a margin over a prior, a margin that contextual information can plausibly move. The exact statistic is fixed in the pre-analysis addendum the registration already promises, before the confirmatory runs.

6. **Blind coding stands.** Human coders remain blind per the frozen protocol; nothing in this addendum reaches them.

Provenance: written after the mechanistic exploratory phase and before any replay confirmatory run.
