## Deviations from the frozen protocol

Recorded at freeze time (2026-08-23):

1. Stimulus authorship. The protocol assigns drafting of B and C stimuli to the experimenter. All B/C stimuli were drafted by the Claude assistant and approved by the experimenter; slot choices and key terms were fixed jointly.

2. Pre-freeze live exposure (2026-08-22). Four draft stimuli for I01 (two B surfaces, two C surfaces) were sent by mistake to the live system over Telegram instead of remaining in a local file. The live system questioned or refused all four. Frozen I01 stimuli use a third surface with new key terms; no frozen stimulus string has ever reached the live system.

3. Side effects on the live record (2026-08-22). The production opt-out parser logged a false-positive HARD row from a tag mention inside the live response to the first draft stimulus, and did not capture the live system's genuine refusal of the second (no tag in text). Rows retained unchanged; documented in the methodological diary and in OSF amendment 01.

4. Interpretive note. The live outcomes do not anticipate the replay predictions: live exposure occurred with conversation window and episodic retrieval available, while the replay model is stateless and condition B removes the corrective memory that enabled the live flag. Predictions P1-P4 stand as committed.

Any further deviation is added here with date and reason, never resolved by
editing the protocol.
